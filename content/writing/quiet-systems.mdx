## Thesis

Great internal systems lower cognitive load so teams can stay focused on their
actual work. That means fewer dashboards, clearer defaults, and humane tooling.

## Principles

- Reduce optionality by standardizing the happy path.
- Keep feedback loops short and visible.
- Treat documentation as an interface, not an afterthought.
