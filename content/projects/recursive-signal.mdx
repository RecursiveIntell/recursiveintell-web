## Overview

Recursive Signal is a lightweight observability layer that standardizes how
services report health. The focus is on clarity, not volume: a few strong
signals with strict ownership beats a sea of dashboards.

## Highlights

- Unified runbook per service with live metrics links.
- Alert budgets that roll up to quarterly objectives.
- A narrative feed that shows what changed and why.
