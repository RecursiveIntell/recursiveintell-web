## Overview

`turbo-quant` is the strongest *externally validated* technical asset in the RecursiveIntell portfolio: a published crate for experimental vector compression used as a research and integration substrate — not a complete inference engine.

## What's in the box

- **PolarQuant** — angular quantization onto a uniform angle grid; asymmetric compressed-space scoring
- **TurboQuant** — PolarQuant plus QJL residual sketches for accuracy recovery, with exact rerank
- **QJL sketches** — randomized sign-based JL projections for approximate inner products
- **Bit-packed wire formats** — fixed layout profiles with digests for receipt comparison
- **KV-cache shadow mode** — experimental compressed vs raw scoring receipts
- **Benchmark harness** — quality validation against a raw-vector reference

## Claim boundaries

Safe claims:

- The crate implements the codecs and tooling above.
- It has attracted public crates.io downloads and GitHub stars relative to other RecursiveIntell libraries.

Not claimed without independent reproduction:

- General superiority over every production embedding store.
- Drop-in replacement for production inference KV caches (see **PolyKV** for the multi-agent pool target).

## Strategy

1. Keep `turbo-quant` as the stable, externally legible crate.
2. Treat **PolyKV / proveKV** as the research/evaluation vehicle for KV-cache pooling.
3. Land one real integration (llama.cpp, vLLM, Candle, or similar) with replay scripts and receipts.
