date: "2024-11-02"
summary: "A structured prompt for turning raw notes into a clear sprint brief."
tags: ["prompt", "planning"]
---

Use this prompt before sprint planning to extract goals, risks, and decision
points from messy notes.

1. Summarize the objective in one sentence.
2. Identify the top three risks.
3. Define the smallest shippable milestone.
