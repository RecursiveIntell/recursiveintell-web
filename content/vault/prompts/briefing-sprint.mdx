## Purpose

Before sprint planning, you often have scattered notes, stakeholder requests, and half-formed ideas. This prompt extracts structure from chaos, producing a one-page brief that aligns the team.

## The Prompt

```
I have rough notes from various sources about what we need to work on. Help me turn these into a clear sprint brief.

Here are my notes:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES]

Please produce a sprint brief with:

1. **Sprint Objective** (1 sentence)
   What is the single most important outcome we're trying to achieve?

2. **Key Deliverables** (3-5 items)
   Concrete things we will ship, with clear done criteria.

3. **Dependencies & Blockers**
   What do we need from others? What might get in our way?

4. **Top Risks** (ranked)
   What could go wrong? What are we uncertain about?

5. **Out of Scope**
   What are we explicitly NOT doing this sprint?

6. **Smallest Shippable Milestone**
   What's the first thing we can deploy that proves progress?

Keep it concise. Each section should be scannable in under 30 seconds.
```

## When to Use

- Start of each sprint planning session
- When stakeholders dump requests on you
- When returning from vacation to a full inbox
- Before a team sync where you need to present priorities

## Tips

- Include raw notes even if messy—the LLM handles ambiguity well
- If the brief doesn't feel right, paste it back with "What's missing?"
- Save good briefs as examples for future sprints

## Variations

**For solo work:**
Replace "we" with "I" and remove the team-oriented sections.

**For longer planning horizons:**
Add sections for "Quarter Context" and "Success Metrics."
